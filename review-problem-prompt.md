# Daily review-problem prompt (paste-in every session)

*Companion to `dsp-audio-plan.md` and `cards.md`. Runs alongside the ~5-min cards pass, not
instead of it — cards stay for atomic recall (facts, sign conventions, small identities); this
is for derivation fluency, which a card can't test. Budget **~10–15 min, one problem**, pulled
from a non-adjacent earlier week. Weight the pick toward whichever week has gone longest
untouched, or whichever concept has a logged error pattern in `LOG.md` or `cards.md`. Solutions
from the archived pset PDFs in `source/` aren't needed for selection, only for your own
self-check after.*

> I'm doing today's review problem, not new material. Below the line is one problem statement
> from an earlier, non-adjacent week — **[name the week, or paste the statement directly]**.
> Today's plan position is **[current Week/Day]**.
>
> Work it exactly like a new pset: full derivation, one Socratic question at a time, restate the
> full problem before the first question so I can write it down. No shortcuts because I've "done
> this before" — if I stall, ask a question that lets me find the next move myself rather than
> stating it. Don't tell me whether I'm right or wrong until I've committed to an answer.
>
> Keep this to ~10–15 min: if it's not close to done by then, note where I stopped and close it
> rather than letting it run into today's new material. Name the concept the problem exercises
> in one clause, so a miss can become a `cards.md` entry the normal way.

**The line you never cross:** this re-derives something already covered, it does not teach
ahead, and it does not displace the day's new-material time. If a pulled problem turns out to
need content past today's plan position, drop it and pick another.

## Picking the problem each day

Rotate through weeks rather than defaulting to whatever's freshest — keep a rough sense of which
week hasn't come up in a while (or check `LOG.md`) and weight toward that, or toward a concept
`LOG.md` has flagged as a recurring error. Content that's really just an atomic fact (a sign
convention, a small identity) belongs in `cards.md`, not here — save this slot for things that
are actual derivations.

## Why this is daily, not checkpoint-only

Checkpoints (the ✅ lines closing DSP 501–505, Week 16, Week 28) still run their own larger
full-chain or full-deck passes — those are unchanged. This is a smaller, daily version of the
same idea: cards keep atomic recall alive cheaply every session, this keeps derivation fluency
alive the same way, at a fixed ~10–15 min cost instead of a variable one.

**Named tradeoff, not hidden:** this is real time against a plan whose own top-listed risk is
pace, currently paced at 3 study days/week with three days of slack. ~10–15 min/day is a
deliberate cap to keep that cost small and bounded rather than open-ended. Worth re-checking
against measured throughput at the Week 16 re-measure day, same as the rest of the pace budget.
